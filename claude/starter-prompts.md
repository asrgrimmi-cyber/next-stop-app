# Starter prompts

**Check the connection**
> What's on my board today?

**Book one ticket**
> Work flight: renew the customer licence, due Monday 5 pm, done when the licence is emailed. Steps: check expiry, generate licence, send it.

**Turn notes into tickets**
> Here are my notes from today's meeting: … Turn every action item into a flight with steps and deadlines. Show me the list before booking.

**Plan the week**
> Plan my week: show what's due each day and anything already late.

**While working**
> Starting WK104.  ·  Done with step 2 on WK104.  ·  Stopped at: waiting for the customer's config.  ·  Finished WK104.

**Reschedule**
> Push everything due today to tomorrow 10 am, except express flights.

**Weekly review**
> What did I depart this week, what slipped, and what should I drop?

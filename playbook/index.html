The idea 01

FLIGHT
Flight = task

Has a line (category), a departure (deadline) and stops.

ONE TRIP
Stop = ticket

A sub-step. You tear it when it is done.

BOARDED
Boarding = starting

Chime, 5-4-3-2-1, focus timer. Stops procrastination.

LAST CALL
Last call = nudge

15 minutes before a stop is due and you have not started.

DEPARTED
Departing = done

Tear the boarding pass. Logged in the travel log.

Do & don't 07

  • Put every time, code and status on tiles or in Doto.
  • One red call to action per screen or slide.
  • Use a line colour for anything category-specific.
  • Show the five-colour stripe under every header.
  • Keep tickets as tickets: notches, perforation, stub.
  • No amber text on the light floor.
  • No new accent colours.
  • No plain rounded cards for tickets.
  • No exclamation marks or emoji in UI copy.
  • No white text on Gym green or Home orange at small sizes; it is under 4.5:1 contrast.
